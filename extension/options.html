Instagram Pet Bridge

Your Instagram DM page → a little friend on your Mac

Keep a logged-in Instagram DM page open. Only new, unambiguous DOM changes after the initial baseline are eligible. Closed/discarded tabs, a closed browser, sleep, and unsupported Instagram layouts cannot be monitored. Inbox observations are previews.

1. Choose the sender

An empty list sends nothing. All processing stays on this Mac.

2. Verify a one-to-one conversation

Open the conversation. Its address ends in /direct/t/THREAD_ID/. Confirm the participant's actual profile username before binding. Binding supplies identity when Instagram exposes only display names. Group conversations are unsupported.

Diagnostics

Logs contain counts and adapter state, never message text. Diagnostics expire after two minutes without a tab heartbeat. “Observing” means a candidate layout was recognized; it does not guarantee delivery.



Advanced: calibrate after Instagram changes its DOM
Inspect a DM row in Chrome. Supply selectors that match only the intended elements. An incoming selector must exclude every outgoing row. A wrong selector can misclassify a message. Test with a new DM and an outgoing message before normal use.
Supported keys: threadRoot, messageRow, incomingMessage, outgoingMessage, messageText, inboxRow, inboxUnread, inboxPreview. Leave unspecified keys out. Example syntax only: {"messageRow":"[role='row']","incomingMessage":".verified-incoming-row","messageText":".verified-body"}. Replace example classes with selectors verified in your own DOM.




No Instagram password, cookies, private API, remote server, or analytics. Pending message text is eligible for local retry for five minutes and is removed when the worker next wakes; the native queue has its own documented retention.
